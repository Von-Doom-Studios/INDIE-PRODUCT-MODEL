## What This Folder Is

This is the indie product portfolio for VonDoom Studios. We are building a stack of niche, single-purpose AI products modeled on [@levelsio](https://x.com/levelsio)'s playbook — Photo AI, Interior AI, Remote OK, Nomads, Vibe Jam. One operator. No team. Charge from day one. Ship ugly and fix in public.

This folder is the single source of truth. Anyone — agent or human — should be able to open `01-GETTING-STARTED.md`, read for 10 minutes, and operate the portfolio at 70% capacity.

## Ground Rules

These are non-negotiable. If a product violates one, kill it or fix it before launch.

1. **One person can build and run it.** No team required. If it can't be solo-operated, it doesn't ship.
2. **Niche before feature.** Target a specific audience with a specific pain. Not "AI for everyone."
3. **Charge from day one.** No free-then-monetize. Paywall on launch, even if it's $9.
4. **Web only at launch.** No native apps. Mobile-responsive web is the floor.
5. **Boring stack.** Use what's stable and what we know. No framework chasing.
6. **Ship before it's ready.** 70% done with the core loop working > polished and unlaunched.
7. **Visible output.** The product must produce something the user wants to share. That's the marketing.
8. **Anchor the price against the human alternative.** "$29/mo vs $500 photoshoot" beats "$29/mo for 100 credits."
9. **Two weeks to MVP, or scope down.** If the MVP needs longer, the scope is wrong.
10. **Public revenue, public progress.** Build in public. Revenue posts, screenshots, behind-the-scenes — that's the growth engine.
