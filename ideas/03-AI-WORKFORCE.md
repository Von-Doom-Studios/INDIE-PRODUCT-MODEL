# Industry AI Workforce

> 100+ pre-trained AI agents that turn one employee into a high-performing team.

## Status: 💡 Concept

## The Problem

Small businesses in security and construction:
- Can't afford full support teams
- Waste hours on admin (emails, reports, bids)
- Lose contracts because response time is slow
- Struggle with CRM, data, and operations

## The Solution

A platform with pre-trained AI agents that handle:
- **Research Agent** — Scans sources, compiles insights, delivers summaries
- **Reporting Agent** — Generates reports from live data on schedule
- **Email Agent** — Drafts, sends, and follows up on communications
- **Sales Agent** — Qualifies leads, updates CRM, tracks pipeline
- **Data Agent** — Cleans, transforms, and visualizes datasets
- **Operations Agent** — Manages workflows, flags bottlenecks, optimizes

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Small security companies (10-50 employees)
**Secondary:** Small construction firms
**Tertiary:** Other service businesses with similar needs

## Why Security/Construction?

- Fragmented industry with many small players
- High admin burden (contracts, RFPs, compliance)
- Not tech-native (low competition from AI tools)
- B2B = higher willingness to pay

## Core Features

### MVP
- [ ] 3-5 core agents (Email, Research, Reporting)
- [ ] Simple chat interface to invoke agents
- [ ] Connect to email (Gmail/Outlook)
- [ ] Basic CRM integration (HubSpot, Pipedrive)
- [ ] Report generation (PDF output)

### V2
- [ ] Contract/RFP analysis and drafting
- [ ] Bid preparation assistant
- [ ] Compliance tracking
- [ ] Full agent marketplace (100+)
- [ ] Custom agent training

## Monetization

| Tier | Price | Agents | Users |
|------|-------|--------|-------|
| Starter | $99/mo | 5 agents | 2 |
| Pro | $299/mo | 20 agents | 10 |
| Enterprise | Custom | All agents | Unlimited |

**Note:** B2B pricing — these businesses pay for results.

## Technical Approach

- **Agents:** GPT-4 with function calling, specialized prompts
- **Integrations:** Gmail API, CRM APIs, calendar
- **Stack:** Next.js + Supabase + Vercel
- **Reports:** Puppeteer or React-PDF for generation

## Competitive Landscape

| Competitor | Weakness |
|------------|----------|
| Generic AI tools (ChatGPT) | Not specialized, no integrations |
| Industry software | Expensive, complex, not AI-native |
| Agencies/VAs | Expensive, inconsistent |

**Our angle:** Pre-trained for your industry, integrates with your tools, works 24/7.

## Risks

- Integration complexity (many CRMs, email providers)
- Industry-specific knowledge required
- Sales cycle may be longer (B2B)
- Support burden from less tech-savvy users
