| ✅ Validated | Confirmed demand, ready to build |

| ❌ Killed | Decided not to pursue |

## Evaluation Criteria

When assessing ideas, score against our [thesis principles](../thesis/01-CORE-PRINCIPLES.md):

1. **Niche clarity** — Is the target audience specific?
2. **Pain intensity** — How much do they need this?
3. **Build simplicity** — Can we MVP in 2 weeks?
4. **Revenue path** — How do we charge?
5. **AI leverage** — Does AI provide 10x value?
