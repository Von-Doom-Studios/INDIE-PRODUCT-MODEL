# Core Principles

> The non-negotiable rules every product must follow.

## 1. Niche Over Broad

**Target a specific audience with a specific problem.**

- ❌ "AI photo editor for everyone"
- ✅ "AI headshots for dating profiles"

Niches are easier to market, easier to dominate, and customers pay more for specialized solutions.

## 2. Simple Over Complex

**If it takes more than 2 weeks to MVP, scope down.**

- Single-purpose tools beat feature-bloated platforms
- One core workflow, done extremely well
- Features come from paying customer requests, not assumptions

## 3. Revenue From Day One

**Charge money immediately.**

- No "build audience first" — validate with wallets
- Pricing proves value; free users prove nothing
- Even $10 tells you more than 10,000 signups

## 4. Solo-Operable

**One person should be able to run it.**

- Automate support where possible
- Minimize infrastructure complexity
- If it needs a team to maintain, it's too complex

## 5. AI as Leverage, Not Gimmick

**AI should 10x the user's capability.**

- Replace expensive professional services (photographers, designers, consultants)
- Turn hours of work into seconds
- Don't use AI just because it's trendy

## 6. Ship Fast, Iterate Faster

**Launch ugly, improve publicly.**

- Version 1 should embarrass you slightly
- Real users provide real feedback
- Perfect is the enemy of profitable
