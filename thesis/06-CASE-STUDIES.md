# Case Studies

> Analysis of successful indie AI products (levelsio portfolio).

## Photo AI — $100K/mo

**What it does:** AI-generated photos of yourself for social media, dating, professional use.

**Niche:** People who need professional-looking photos without hiring a photographer.

**Why it works:**
- Clear pain point: Professional photos are expensive ($200-500+)
- AI delivers 10x value at 10% cost
- Viral potential (people share their AI photos)
- Multiple use cases (dating, LinkedIn, Instagram)

**Key patterns:**
- Extensive onboarding (upload photos, train model)
- Photo packs (pre-designed prompts for specific use cases)
- Freemium: free credits to try, pay for more
- Upsells: video, higher quality, more models

**Takeaways:**
- Bundle complexity into "packs" for easy UX
- Let users try before paying (free tier)
- Multiple monetization paths (credits, packs, subscription)

## Interior AI — $35K/mo

**What it does:** Upload a photo of a room, AI redesigns it in any style.

**Niche:** Homeowners, real estate agents, interior design enthusiasts.

**Why it works:**
- Interior designers charge $2,000-10,000+
- Visual transformation = instant "wow" factor
- Clear before/after = shareable content
- B2B angle (real estate staging)

**Key patterns:**
- Style presets (Modern, Scandinavian, etc.)
- Room type selection for better results
- Virtual staging mode for empty rooms
- Sketch-to-render for architects

**Takeaways:**
- Presets make AI accessible to non-technical users
- B2B use cases often pay more (real estate agents)
- Before/after format is inherently viral

## Vibe Jam — $39K/mo

**What it does:** AI game development competition (90%+ code written by AI).

**Niche:** Developers interested in AI-assisted coding.

**Why it works:**
- Taps into AI coding hype (Cursor, Copilot)
- Community-driven (participants promote it)
- Creates content (games showcase the platform)
- Prize money drives participation

**Key patterns:**
- Clear rules and structure
- Widget tracking for submissions
- Public voting/judging
- Portal system connecting games (webring)

**Takeaways:**
- Competitions create urgency and community
- Participants become marketers
- Platform benefits from user-generated content

## Remote OK — $44K/mo

**What it does:** Remote job board.

**Niche:** Remote workers and companies hiring remote.

**Why it works:**
- First-mover in remote jobs niche
- Companies pay to post (B2B revenue)
- SEO-driven organic traffic
- Simple, focused product

**Key patterns:**
- Companies pay to post (~$300+)
- SEO-optimized job listings
- Email job alerts
- Salary transparency

**Takeaways:**
- Job boards are high-margin (companies pay, workers use free)
- First-mover + SEO = durable advantage
- Niche job boards beat generic ones

## Common Patterns Across All Products

### 1. Niche Positioning
Every product targets a specific audience with a specific problem.

### 2. Clear Value Proposition
One sentence explains what it does and why you'd pay.

### 3. Visual Results
Output is immediately visible and shareable.

### 4. Multiple Revenue Streams
Credits, subscriptions, and one-time purchases coexist.

### 5. Solo-Operable
All products can be (and largely are) run by one person.

### 6. AI as Cost Reducer
AI replaces expensive professional services.
