# Monetization

> Pricing strategies and revenue models.

## Core Models

### 1. Credits / Usage-Based
**Best for:** AI generation tools (images, text, video)

```
$X buys Y credits
Each generation costs Z credits
```

**Pros:** Fair, scales with value
**Cons:** Unpredictable revenue, complex UX

**Examples:** Photo AI, Midjourney, most AI tools

### 2. Subscription
**Best for:** Tools used regularly, ongoing value

```
$X/month for unlimited (or high-limit) usage
```

**Pros:** Predictable revenue, customer commitment
**Cons:** Harder to convert, churn risk

**Examples:** Interior AI Pro, Nomad List

### 3. One-Time Purchase
**Best for:** Single deliverable (report, pack, one-off service)

```
$X for [specific output]
```

**Pros:** Simple, no ongoing relationship
**Cons:** Need constant new customers

### 4. Hybrid (Recommended)
**Best for:** Most indie AI products

```
Free tier: X free credits/generations
Paid: Subscription OR credit packs
```

**Why it works:**
- Free tier drives word-of-mouth
- Multiple conversion paths
- Captures both casual and power users

## Pricing Principles

### 1. Start Higher Than Comfortable
- You can always lower prices
- Raising prices is harder
- High prices filter for serious users

### 2. Anchor Against Alternatives
- "Professional photoshoot: $500. Photo AI: $29/mo"
- "Interior designer: $2,000. Interior AI: $39/mo"
- Show the 10x-100x value gap

### 3. Simple Tiers
- Maximum 3 pricing tiers
- Clear differentiation
- One obvious "best value" option

| Tier | Price | Limit |
|------|-------|-------|
| Free | $0 | 5/month |
| Pro | $29/mo | 200/month |
| Unlimited | $99/mo | Unlimited |

## Payment Psychology

- **Annual discounts:** 2 months free drives commitment
- **Round numbers:** $29 > $28.99 for premium positioning
- **Show savings:** "Save $58/year" on annual plans

## Revenue Milestones

| Stage | MRR Target | Focus |
|-------|------------|-------|
| Validation | $100 | Prove someone will pay |
| Ramen | $2,000 | Basic expenses covered |
| Sustainable | $5,000 | Comfortable solo income |
| Scaling | $10,000+ | Consider team/adjacent products |
