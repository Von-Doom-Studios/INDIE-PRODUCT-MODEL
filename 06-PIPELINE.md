## Stage Definitions

| Stage | What "done" looks like to advance |
|---|---|
| 💡 Concept | Idea captured in `ideas/` file with full thesis-fit scoring. |
| 🔍 Researching | 5+ user interviews complete. Competitor map done. Demand signals documented. |
| ✅ Validated | Either: paid waitlist with ≥20 signups, OR pre-sales ≥$500, OR clear letters of intent. |
| 🚧 Building | MVP in development. Daily commits. Public build-in-progress posts. |
| 🚀 Launched | Stripe live. ≥1 paying customer. Public launch tweet/post done. |
| ⏸️ Paused | Not killed, but no work this cycle. Reason logged. |
| ❌ Killed | Reason logged. File moved to `ideas/killed/` for record. |

## Validation Bar

An idea cannot become "Building" unless validation produces one of:
1. **20+ paid waitlist signups** (e.g. $5 deposit), OR
2. **$500+ in pre-sales**, OR
3. **3+ letters of intent** from B2B targets

No validation → no build. This is the discipline.
